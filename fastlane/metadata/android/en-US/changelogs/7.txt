WhisperLoom 3.4.0 – voice tasks for your own agent.
• New home screen widget: tap, speak, tap — WhisperLoom transcribes and sends the task to a server you run yourself
• Four states with a running timer; tapping an error resends the same task without speaking again
• Keeps going with the screen off or through a short loss of signal
• Settings → Advanced options. Off by default
