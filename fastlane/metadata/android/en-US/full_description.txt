WhisperLoom types for you: tap, speak, done – the recognised text lands right in the active text field of any app.

Three ways to dictate:
• A floating microphone button above all apps (your keyboard stays as it is). Tap to record, tap again to insert, drag to ✕ to discard.
• A dictation keyboard with hold-to-talk for apps that block overlays. While recording, swipe right to lock – recording continues hands-free, then send or discard; swipe left to discard right away. The wand above the microphone opens the four text levels without leaving the app.
• A home screen widget for voice tasks to your own agent (see below). Off by default.

Recognition your way:
• Online with your own account at OpenAI, Groq (free tier), Mistral, Together, DeepInfra, OpenRouter, or any OpenAI-compatible server you host yourself (speaches, whisper.cpp server).
• Offline on the device with whisper.cpp. Models (Tiny to Large v3 Turbo) are downloaded on demand – none is bundled.
• Vocabulary for names and technical terms: as a list in the app or as a linked .md/.txt file that is re-read with every dictation – edits to the file take effect right away.

Text the way you want it:
• Remove filler words (editable list), automatic capitalisation.
• Optional clean-up by a language model in three levels: polish, rephrase, summarise – using your own or the recognition account, including Ollama: locally on your home server without a key (the text never leaves your network) or via Ollama Cloud. The model list comes straight from the server.
• For AI users, switchable in the advanced options: the "Prompt" level turns a dictation into a well-structured prompt for ChatGPT, Claude & co.
• "Automatic paragraphs" switch: the AI splits longer dictations into paragraphs – or, when switched off, returns everything as one continuous text.

Transcribe voice messages: share a message from WhatsApp, Telegram or Signal → WhisperLoom. The text appears in readable paragraphs, verbatim or without fillers, ready to copy or forward.

Voice tasks for your own agent: tap, speak, tap – WhisperLoom transcribes and sends the task to a server you run yourself; the reply arrives wherever you normally message your agent. Delivery keeps going with the screen off. Requires your own server (the open-source counterpart is on GitHub) and stays invisible until you set it up.

Privacy: your API key never leaves the device. Online, only the audio goes to the provider you chose; offline, nothing leaves the phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
